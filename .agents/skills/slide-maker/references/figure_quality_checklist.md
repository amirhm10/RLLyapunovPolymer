# Figure Quality Checklist

Use this before adding a figure to a slide deck.

## Scientific checks

- Does the figure answer a slide question
- Is the comparison fair
- Are the baselines and variants labeled clearly
- Do the axes, units, and normalization choices match the manuscript

## Slide readability checks

- Are fonts large enough for projection
- Are legend labels short
- Are setpoints, outputs, and bounds visually distinct
- Are line styles readable in grayscale
- Is the crop tight enough to remove wasted space

## RL and MPC checks

- Show setpoints explicitly for tracking plots
- Show bounds for manipulated inputs when they matter
- Distinguish training reward from evaluation tracking
- Label nominal, fluctuation, ramp, and last-episode cases clearly
- State whether the plotted run is a single evaluation, mean over seeds, or both
