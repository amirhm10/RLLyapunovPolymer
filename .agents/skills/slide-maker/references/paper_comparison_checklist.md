# Paper Comparison Checklist

Use this when a slide compares the repo's method to prior work.

## Before writing the slide

- Read the cited paper section or a reliable metadata source
- Identify what problem the paper solves
- Identify what method the paper uses
- Identify what assumptions or safety mechanisms it relies on

## On the slide

- Say what is similar
- Say what is different
- Say what this repo contributes beyond the prior work
- Cite the source directly

## Avoid

- claiming stronger performance without matched conditions
- implying deployment evidence when the paper only reports simulation
- copying copyrighted plots when a table or schematic would do
