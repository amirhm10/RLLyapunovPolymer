# PhD Research Story Structure

Use this when a talk needs to sound like research, not like a pasted report.

## Core arc

1. Why this process-control problem matters
2. Why the current baseline is not enough
3. What method change is proposed
4. Why that change is technically sensible
5. What the evidence shows
6. What is still limited
7. What the next experiment or deployment step should be

## Good slide roles for this repo

- Motivation from nonlinear, drifting, or high-cost process operation
- Gap between fixed OF-MPC and practical online adaptation
- Method slide for MPC-pretrained TD3, RL-assisted MPC, or Lyapunov-filtered safe RL
- Implementation slide for polymer reactor or Aspen Dynamics `C_2` splitter
- Main comparison results
- Interpretation slide that explains why the method improved
- Limitation and next-step slide
- Backup slides for equations, hyperparameters, and extra scenarios

## Useful timing heuristics

- 3-minute talk: 1 title slide, 2 problem/method slides, 1 result slide, 1 takeaway slide
- 10 to 15 minute talk: 8 to 12 main slides plus backup
- Committee or defense update: 12 to 20 main slides plus backup
