# Slide Quality Checklist

Use this as a final pass before calling a deck presentation-ready.

## Story

- Does each slide have one clear message
- Does each title state a conclusion when possible
- Does the deck move from problem to method to evidence to interpretation
- Is there a visible limitation or next-step slide when the work is incomplete

## Evidence

- Is every technical claim backed by a plot, table, equation, or citation
- Are baselines named clearly
- Are comparisons fair
- Are weak results described honestly

## Visuals

- Can the smallest axis label be read from a room
- Are legends short and unambiguous
- Are figure colors and line styles distinguishable in grayscale
- Is the slide free of clutter and tiny text

## Delivery

- Can the slide be explained aloud in 30 to 90 seconds
- Is backup material separated from the main narrative
- Does the final slide say what happens next
